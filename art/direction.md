# Art direction

- Style: glossy vinyl toy / Nintendo-like blind-box chibi; chunky beveled forms; clean surfaces.
- Key art: art/key-art.png (from the asset pack). Every asset follows it.
- Main colors: cream floor tile #efe3cc, slate-blue floor tile #8fa3b8, crate orange #e8862e,
  bumper teal #2fb3ad with silver #c9ccd3.
- Player: red #e8322f and white. Bots: blue, green, yellow versions of the same bomber.
- Danger: blast flames hot yellow-white core to orange #ff8a1f; bomb fuse blinking red.
- Reward: power-ups float, spin and glint above their tile with a soft pale-yellow ring.
- Light: bright late morning, warm grade, soft shadows straight under objects (top-down readable).
- Camera: fixed perspective, about 50° down, whole arena in view.
- HUD: rounded candy-like pill cards, thick white type with dark outline, one card per bomber
  across the top with its power-up counts; centred round timer.
- Type: system rounded sans, heavy weight.
- Motifs: rounded pills, the red-white helmet stripe.
- Sound: bouncy toy pops and chunky booms (ZzFX).
